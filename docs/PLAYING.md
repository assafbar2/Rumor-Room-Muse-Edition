# How to play Rumor Room

Four claims. Four research turns. One claim that doesn't hold up.

Choose any of the ten case files. Read the briefing, then decide which story the evidence contradicts. Everything takes place inside the fictional case: you do not need to search the web or know anything about real celebrities, companies, wine, or fashion.

## Follow a lead

Select a claim card. Spend one research turn on a move:

| Move                 | What it looks for                                             |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Trace the leak**   | The earliest available source of the story                    |
| **Second source**    | Other accounts, including whether they repeat the same origin |
| **Official channel** | A relevant first-party record                                 |
| **Fresh cut**        | A later update within the case's time window                  |

You have four research turns for the whole case. You can use a move on different claims, but not repeat the same claim/move pair. A search that finds no update still spends one turn. New documents appear under **Evidence**; opening and re-reading them is always free.

Compare the actual words, times, identifiers, and origin groups. Three articles repeating one screenshot are still one underlying source.

## Look closer and ask better

The **Exhibit** tab has a prepared image, a crop, and an exact text transcript. Enlarge it, compare the small details, and pin it to your file for free. Finding the related record unlocks the comparison note.

The **Witness** tab offers five prepared questions. Choose up to two. These interviews don't cost research turns or reduce your score. Treat testimony as a lead to compare with documents.

All ten cases are fully playable with the prepared questions. A server deployment may offer explicitly labeled live AI questions; the GitHub Pages game does not need them.

## Make your verdict

Select **Make my verdict**, choose the claim you don't buy, and confirm with **Lock my verdict**. You can accuse at any time, including before researching or after spending all four turns. Confirmation closes that run.

The receipt shows the correct answer, decisive citations, the verification lesson, and your investigation. **Your research** lists documents you actually discovered; **Revealed at verdict** lists evidence shown only after you decided. If your pick was wrong, the receipt also shows the records supporting it.

## Score

A correct verdict earns **100 points plus 15 for each unused research turn**, up to **160**. An incorrect verdict scores **0**.

For example, two research moves followed by the correct verdict earns **130**. Interviews and image inspection do not change the score. An immediate lucky guess can earn 160; the receipt plainly shows that you used no research.

## Save, replay, and controls

- Progress saves automatically on this browser when storage is available. You can leave one case, play another, and return.
- **Restart** asks before resetting the current investigation. **Replay this case** opens a fresh run after a verdict. Both keep your best score and the other cases.
- Solutions stay the same on replay. The order of the four claims changes.
- **Reset my progress** asks before clearing all ten case saves and best scores on this browser.
- Use Tab and Enter/Space to reach and activate controls. Arrow keys, Home, and End switch the desk tabs. Escape closes a dialog and returns focus. Use the skip link to reach the game directly.
- If storage is unavailable, you can still play for the current visit. Saves do not move between devices or sites.
